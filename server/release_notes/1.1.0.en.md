Split documents by drag & drop ✂️
• Multi-file view: select several pages with Shift / Ctrl(⌘) + click or "Select all"
• Drag the selection onto the "Drop pages here" card to split it into a new file (e.g. pages 10–20 of a 20-page file)
• "Split into new file…" button: type a range such as 10-20 or 1,3,5-7
• Drag several pages at once to another file or reorder them (hold Alt/⌥ to copy)
• New files are named automatically, e.g. report-p10-20.pdf
• Right-click a page (thumbnails and multi-file view): duplicate · cut · copy · paste · rotate · delete · split — cut/copy and paste across files · shortcuts Ctrl/⌘ + D X C V and Delete
